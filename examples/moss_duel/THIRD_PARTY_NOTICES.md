Exact download URLs, source archive hashes, file sizes, and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `assets/asset_manifest.json`. Only the listed sound files and the original licences are bundled; the source archives and preview recordings are omitted.

The characters, arena, effects and interface are drawn specifically for this example. It does not include Hollow Knight assets. The CC0 terms apply to these third-party sounds; they do not assign a licence to the surrounding repository or original game code.
